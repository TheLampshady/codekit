# Decision Log

What was decided and why, one entry per shipped ticket, newest first, for reference when someone asks why something was built a certain way.

**AI-extracted, and it may be wrong.** The codekit update writes each entry after merge from the spec documents on the ticket's Linear issue. The linked ticket is the source: check it before relying on an entry. Entries are never edited, and a later decision says which earlier one it replaces.
